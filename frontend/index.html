How It Works
The Problem

Cash locked in unpaid invoices

Small and medium-sized businesses often deliver goods or services, issue a legitimate invoice, and still wait weeks or months for payment. Working capital stays tied up — making it harder to pay employees, suppliers, buy materials, or take on new contracts.

The Solution

Evidence-backed Trust Profiles

An SME uploads an invoice and supporting evidence such as a purchase order or proof of delivery. FlowTrust analyses document consistency, buyer information, delivery evidence and historical payment behaviour to generate an explainable Receivable Trust Profile — so funders can assess legitimate invoices faster.

Who We Are

About FlowTrust

An independent verification layer — not a lender.

01

Our Mission

To close the gap between completed work and cash in the bank for SMEs by turning invoices into structured, evidence-backed Trust Profiles that funders can trust.

02

Our Approach

We combine invoice, buyer, delivery and payment signals into a transparent Receivable Trust Profile. We do not guarantee payment or make the final lending decision — we provide the evidence and risk insights that support it.

03

Our Advantage

A growing Receivable Trust Network: independent, reusable verification profiles and proprietary transaction intelligence that improve as more SMEs, buyers and funders participate.

What We Do

Services

Verification and intelligence that make receivables finance-ready.

Invoice & Evidence Upload

SMEs upload invoices plus supporting documents — purchase orders, proof of delivery, delivery notes and related records — in one secure portal.

Multi-Signal Analysis

We analyse document consistency, buyer information, buyer confirmation, delivery evidence and historical payment behaviour to build a clear picture of the receivable.

Receivable Trust Profile

An explainable Trust Profile for each receivable — structured evidence and risk insights that help funders assess legitimate invoices faster, without FlowTrust guaranteeing payment.

Funder Decision Support

Banks, invoice-finance providers, fintech lenders and development-finance institutions use our profiles to reduce manual verification and speed up decisions on working-capital facilities.
